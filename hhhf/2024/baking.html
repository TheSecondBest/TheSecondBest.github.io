The bake-off is on again this year!

Bake a Christmas goody for Christmas Eve, where all the baked goods will be judged. Your treat doesn't need to be Christmasy, specifically, but it does need to be something you traditionally bake at Christmastime (Christmastide?!)



The goodies will be judged on the following criteria:

–Presentation (size, appearance, etc)

–Holiday Spirit

–Taste
