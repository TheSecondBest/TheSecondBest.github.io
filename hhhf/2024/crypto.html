A cryptogram is a coded message using a substitution cypher, one alphabet letter for another. To figure out the message, you need to work out which letter of the alphabet is being substituted by each coded letter. No letter will stand for itself or for more than one other letter. Look for common words such as THE, AND, A, I, etc. Also look for common word endings like E, S, ING, a letter after an apostrophe could be S, T, or D, etc. It is helpful to write the uncoded letter under the coded one. Use pencil so you can erase if you are wrong.


Example:

JINGLE BELLS

ALBCRS JSRRT
