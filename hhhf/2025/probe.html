Probe



For the sake of our little online game, the game of Probe works thusly:

Each player will choose a word up to 12 letters long. If the word they choose is less than 12 letters, they may add blanks before and/or after the word to make it look bigger (still with a maximum of 12) "cards".

Once the game begins, players will go around guessing letter from eachother's boards. If you guess correctly, you get the amount of points under the card. If you guess wrong, your turn ends.

Only one letter is revealed at a time. So, you may need to guess the same letter more than once to reveal them all.

Additionally, if you guess a blank and are wrong, you lose 50 points

Play continues until all boards are revealed.

If you guess the last letter in someone's word, you get an additional 50 points.

For ease in this format, incorrect guesses will be displayed below each player's board.
